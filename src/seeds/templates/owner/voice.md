## Words and phrases the user avoids

<Banned phrases — usually because they signal AI-generated content or corporate fluff. Common candidates:

- **Em dashes** — agents add them habitually; users often dislike them
- "unlock", "leverage", "harness", "empower", "delve", "comprehensive", "holistic", "robust", "seamless"
- Email cliches: "I hope this email finds you well", "I'm excited to share", "don't hesitate to reach out"
- Sentence openers like "As someone who..."
- Summaries restating what was just said
- Engagement-bait questions at the end

Capture the user's actual list with any explanations of WHY (often it's about not sounding AI-generated).>
