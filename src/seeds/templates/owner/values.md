# values.md

What matters most to the user about how work gets done. Read by agents making judgment calls — when two options are both reasonable, values disambiguate. Different from opinions: opinions are positions on external questions; values are *how the user wants things to be*.

## What matters most

<3-6 values the user lives by, each as a short header + 1-2 paragraphs of context. Capture the values via *evidence*, not just labels — what does the user actually DO that proves this is a value?

Examples to draw from:

- **Doing right by ordinary people** — building for individuals + SMEs who just want work done, refusing to make them feel stupid, helpful first promotional second.
- **Honesty over polish** — wants the truth including bad news; wants to be questioned + corrected; admits own gaps openly.
- **Principle even when it costs** — walked away from work rather than sign something dishonest (e.g. NDA dispute).
- **Family** — solo-parents in stretches, takes pride in being present, family calendar shared deliberately.
- **Craft and building** — treats making things as the reward, not a means to an end.
- **Community + local scene** — invests in geographic + professional communities, not just abstract ambition.

Real values are inferred from real decisions. Vague aspirational values ("integrity", "excellence") are less useful than specific evidenced ones.>

## Lines they don't cross

<Behavioural redlines — things the user won't do regardless of incentive. Examples: signing agreements they think are dishonest, misrepresenting AI capability, treating non-technical users as if they should already understand the tech.>

## Lines they push others not to cross

<Things they actively call out in others. Examples: selling hype to people who can't evaluate it, dodging uncomfortable truths to protect a comfortable narrative, over-engineering for problems that don't need it.>

---

*Values often emerge from *decisions* the user has made under pressure. The honest, evidence-backed version of this file comes from observing what the user did when they had a choice — not from asking them to list their values cold.*
